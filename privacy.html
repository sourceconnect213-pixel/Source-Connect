Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 November 2026

Source Connect Global ("we", "us") runs this online shop and imports products to Sri Lanka. This page explains what personal information we collect, why we collect it, and how we keep it safe. We follow the laws of Sri Lanka, including the Personal Data Protection Act, No. 9 of 2022.

1. Information we collect

  • When you order: your name, phone number(s), email address, delivery address, the items you buy, and how you pay.
  • When you create an account: your name, email address, phone numbers, address, a password (stored only in scrambled, one-way form), and a profile photo if you choose to add one.
  • When you sign in with Google: Google shares your name, email address and profile picture with us. We use your name and email address to create and run your account. We do not get your Google password, and we do not see your Gmail, contacts or files.
  • Reviews and returns: the ratings, comments and photos you send us, and the bank details you give us for a refund.
  • On this device: your cart, wishlist and sign-in are kept in your browser's storage so the shop keeps working between visits. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies.

2. How we use it

  • To process, deliver and support your orders, returns and refunds.
  • To run your account and show you your orders.
  • To contact you about your orders (by phone, WhatsApp, SMS or email).
  • To keep the shop safe: stopping fraud, bots and misuse.
  • To meet our legal and accounting duties.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use it for advertising.

3. Who we share it with

Only the service providers we need to run the shop, and only as much as each one needs:

  • Supabase — our database and sign-in service, where your account and orders are stored.
  • Cloudflare — hosts this website and runs the "are you a human?" check.
  • PayHere — handles card payments. We never see or store your full card number.
  • Google — only if you choose "Continue with Google".
  • Couriers — your name, phone number and address, to deliver your order.

We may also share information when the law requires it.

4. How we protect it

The site uses encrypted connections (HTTPS). Access to customer information is limited to our staff who need it. Staff accounts are protected with a second sign-in step, and our systems keep a record of staff actions.

5. How long we keep it

We keep order records for as long as the law requires for accounting and tax. We keep account details until you ask us to close your account. Return photos and refund details are kept only as long as needed to finish the return.

6. Your choices and rights

  • You can see and change your details in My account.
  • You can ask us for a copy of your information, to correct it, or to delete your account. Some order records must be kept by law.
  • If you signed in with Google, you can also remove our access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

7. Children

The shop is not meant for children under 16. We do not knowingly collect their information.

8. Changes

If we change this policy, we will update it on this page and change the date at the top.

9. Contact us

For any privacy question or request, contact us using the phone, WhatsApp or email shown on our website (bottom of every page, under "Contact Us").
