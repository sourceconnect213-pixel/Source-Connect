Return & Refund Policy

Last updated: 26 November 2026

We want you to be happy with every order from Source Connect Global. If something is not right, you can ask for a return or refund as explained below.

1. Return period

You can ask for a return within 7 days after your order is delivered.

2. When you can return an item

  • The item arrived damaged or broken.
  • The item is faulty or does not work.
  • You received the wrong item, size or colour, or something is missing.
  • The item is clearly different from its description on our website.
  • Change of mind (including size not suitable): accepted if the item is unused, unwashed, with all tags, accessories and original packaging.

3. Items that cannot be returned

Unless they arrived damaged, faulty or wrong:

  • Underwear, innerwear, swimwear and socks.
  • Cosmetics, personal care and hygiene products that have been opened.
  • Items that have been used, washed, damaged by the customer, or are missing parts or packaging.
  • Items marked "non-returnable" or "final sale" on the product page.

4. How to ask for a return

  1. Open your order: My account → My orders, or the Track order page with your order number.
  2. Press Return / Refund, choose the items, the reason, and add photos of the problem.
  3. Add the bank account where you want the refund (for cash on delivery and bank transfer orders).
  4. We reply within 2 working days and tell you how to send the item back. You can also contact us by phone or WhatsApp.

5. Return delivery cost

  • If the item is damaged, faulty, wrong or not as described, we pay the return delivery.
  • For a change of mind, the customer pays the return delivery, and the original delivery charge is not refunded.

6. Refunds

  • We check the returned item within 3 working days of receiving it.
  • Once approved, the refund is made within 7–14 working days:
    • Card payments: back to the same card through PayHere. Your bank may take a few more days to show it.
    • Cash on delivery and bank transfer: to the bank account you gave us.
  • You can choose a replacement or exchange instead of a refund, if the item is in stock.
  • You can follow every step of your return on your order page.

7. Cancelling an order

You can cancel free of charge before the order is shipped. If you already paid, we refund the full amount as in section 6.

8. Contact us

For help with a return, contact us using the phone, WhatsApp or email shown on our website (bottom of every page, under "Contact Us").
